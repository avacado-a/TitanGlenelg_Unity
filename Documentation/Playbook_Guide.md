# Focused Playbook Guide: Titan-Glenelg Vertical Slice

## 1. Offensive Playbook

### Play 1: Pistol Verticals (Four Verticals)
- **Formation:** Pistol Trips Right
- **Concept:** Stretches defensive coverage vertically across 4 deep lanes.
  - **WR1 (Boundary):** Outside fade down left sideline.
  - **WR2 (Slot):** High-speed seam up the hash. Primary target vs single-high safeties.
  - **WR3 (Field):** Deep go route down right sideline.
  - **TE (In-line):** Seam route bending toward middle field.
  - **RB:** Check-and-release into the flat for checkdown.

### Play 2: Pistol Gladiator Counter Trey
- **Formation:** Pistol Gladiator
- **Concept:** Heavy gap-scheme power run featuring pulling backside linemen.
  - **Left Guard (LG):** Pulls across the formation behind center to kick out the edge defender on line of scrimmage.
  - **Right Tackle (RT):** Wraps through the C-gap to seal the second-level linebacker.
  - **RB:** Counter step left, plants outside foot, and follows pulling blocks through the off-tackle gap.

### Play 3: Pistol Quick Slants
- **Formation:** Pistol Spread 2x2
- **Concept:** Rapid 3-step rhythm pass.
  - **WR1 & WR3:** 3-step vertical stem (~6 yards), sharp 45-degree plant and cut inside across the safety's face.
  - **Slot Receivers:** Flat/arrow routes clearing underneath linebackers.

## 2. Defensive Playbook

### Play 1: Cover 3 Sky
- **Formation:** Base 4-3
- **Concept:** Single-high safety 3-deep zone coverage.
  - **FS:** Deep middle 1/3 defender.
  - **CB Left & CB Right:** Deep outside 1/3 defenders with outside leverage.
  - **Strong Safety (SS):** Sky roll down to strong flat/curl.
  - **Linebackers:** Hook/curl underneath zones.

### Play 2: Cover 2 Man
- **Formation:** Nickel 3-3-5
- **Concept:** Two-high split safety zone with 5 underneath press-man defenders.
  - **FS & SS:** Split deep halves, preventing deep post and fade completions.
  - **Cornerbacks & Nickel DBs:** Press alignment with inside trail leverage, funneling receivers to safety help.

### Play 3: Cover 0 Blitz
- **Formation:** Base 4-3
- **Concept:** All-out 6-man pass rush with zero deep safety help.
  - **Front:** 4 down linemen + 2 blitzing linebackers/safeties through A and B gaps.
  - **Secondary:** Aggressive man-to-man press coverage across all eligible receivers.
